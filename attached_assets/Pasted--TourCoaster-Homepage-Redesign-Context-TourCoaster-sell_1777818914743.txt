# TourCoaster Homepage Redesign

## Context
TourCoaster sells two things:
1. **In-person experiences** — hiking tours, city tours, expert travel guides
2. **VR Tour subscription** — virtual versions of the same tours (for people who can't travel, want to preview, or just love armchair adventure)

The current homepage looks dated. Rebuild it from scratch as a bold, vibrant, energetic travel brand. Think *Outside Magazine* meets *Airbnb Experiences* — editorial, confident, photo-forward. NOT corporate SaaS, NOT sterile minimalism, NOT generic startup template.

## Design system

**Color palette (pick ONE primary, no purple-blue gradients):**
- Primary: deep forest `#0F4C3A` OR sunset orange `#E85D2C` — choose based on which feels more energetic with the imagery
- Background: warm off-white `#FAF7F2` (not pure white)
- Text: `#1A1A1A` (not pure black)
- Single contrasting accent for CTAs only

**Typography:**
- ONE sans-serif family, multiple weights (Inter Tight, Söhne, or General Sans)
- Display sizes BIG: hero headline 96–128px desktop, 56px mobile
- Tight letter-spacing on headlines (-0.02em to -0.04em)
- Generous body line-height (1.5–1.6)

**Imagery:**
- Real travel/outdoor photography, full-bleed, edge-to-edge
- No overlays unless needed for text legibility
- Use Unsplash placeholders with search terms like "hiking landscape", "mountain trail", "city tour" — I'll swap real photos later

## Homepage sections (in this order)

### 1. Hero (95vh)
- Full-bleed dramatic landscape photo (hiker on trail, mountain vista, or vibrant city street)
- Massive bottom-aligned headline overlaying photo: **"Tours worth showing up for. And the ones you can take from your couch."**
- Sub-line: one sentence about both products
- Two CTAs side-by-side: primary "Browse Tours" + secondary "Try VR Free"
- Minimal nav at top: logo left, [Tours, VR, Guides, Sign in] right

### 2. Dual-product split
This is the most important section — surfaces both product lines instantly.
- Two equal columns, full width, no padding between
- LEFT panel: "Go there." — large photo, 2-line description of in-person tours, CTA
- RIGHT panel: "Or go in VR." — different photo, 2-line description of subscription, CTA
- Different background tones for each panel so the split is visually obvious

### 3. Featured tours grid
- 3 or 4 tours, large cards (don't cram — let them breathe)
- Each card: 4:5 photo, location label, tour name, duration, price, "Available in VR" badge where relevant
- Hover: subtle photo zoom (1.05x, 600ms ease-out), nothing else
- "View all 47 tours →" link below grid

### 4. Three reasons / why TourCoaster
- Numbered 01, 02, 03 in large display weight
- Short headline + one paragraph each
- NO icons in colored circles — let typography do the work
- Examples: "Local guides, not tour guides" / "Small groups, never coach loads" / "Every trail, also in VR"

### 5. VR subscription block
- Inverted color (dark background if rest is light)
- Headline: **"Can't travel? We bring the trail to you."**
- Brief explainer + "From $X/month" pricing teaser + CTA to plans page
- Imagery suggestion: stylized first-person POV from a VR tour with a subtle viewfinder hint — avoid clichéd "person wearing headset" shot, it looks dated

### 6. Testimonials
- Maximum 3, real-feeling, with name + tour they took + small photo
- Don't fabricate "Trusted by 10,000+ adventurers" — leave a comment for me to fill in real numbers

### 7. Footer
- Dark, organized columns: Tours / VR / Company / Support
- Single email input + button for newsletter, no checkboxes
- Social icons in a row, monochrome

## Tech requirements
- Next.js + Tailwind CSS
- Mobile-first; test breakpoints at 375px, 768px, 1440px
- `next/image` for all photos with proper sizing
- Lazy-load everything below the fold
- Semantic HTML (`<section>`, `<article>`, `<nav>`, h1–h3 hierarchy)
- WCAG AA contrast minimum
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## DO NOT do these (AI codegen defaults to all of these — please don't)
- ❌ Purple-to-blue gradient anywhere
- ❌ Glassmorphism / frosted glass cards
- ❌ Floating 3D dashboard or app mockup in the hero
- ❌ Three-column "features" row with Lucide icons in colored rounded squares
- ❌ Centered hero text on a flat color background
- ❌ `rounded-3xl` and heavy shadows on everything — keep radii modest (8–12px)
- ❌ Animated blur orbs / gradient meshes in the background
- ❌ Generic CTA copy like "Get started" or "Learn more" — be specific
- ❌ Stock photos of diverse teams pointing at laptops
