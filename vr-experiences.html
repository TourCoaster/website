Accessibility at the Core

Every VR experience on TourCoaster is designed with accessibility as a primary feature, not an afterthought.

  • Keyboard Navigation — Full control without a mouse or controller
  • Audio Descriptions — Detailed narration of every scene
  • One-Hand Mode — Simplified controls for limited mobility
  • Slow-Paced Options — Adjustable movement speed
  • High Contrast — Enhanced visibility for visual impairments
  • 2D Fallback — Photo gallery alternative for low bandwidth
