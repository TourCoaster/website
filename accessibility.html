Accessibility

Tourism should be for everyone. Here's how we make it happen.

Accessibility-First Design

At TourCoaster, accessibility isn't an add-on — it's built into every layer of our platform.

Whether you have mobility challenges, visual or hearing impairments, or simply prefer to explore from home, we've designed multiple pathways to experience every destination.

Our Accessibility Features

Wheelchair-Accessible Routing

Every tour is rated for accessibility. Filter tours by wheelchair access, flat terrain, and elevator availability.

Audio-First Navigation

Detailed audio descriptions for every VR experience, with spatial audio that brings environments to life.

Virtual Mobility VR Mode

Experience tours from a wheelchair perspective in VR, with realistic navigation and viewpoints.

High Contrast Interface

Enhanced visibility mode across all VR experiences and website interfaces for users with visual impairments.

One-Hand Controls

All VR experiences support one-hand navigation mode and keyboard-only operation.

2D Fallback Gallery

Every VR tour has a high-resolution photo gallery alternative for low bandwidth or non-VR users.

Need Specific Accommodations?

We're happy to work with you to ensure your tour experience meets your specific needs.
