About TourCoaster

The accessibility-first global exploration network.

Our Story

TourCoaster began with a simple idea: everyone should be able to explore the world, regardless of their physical abilities, location, or budget.

Founded by a team of 360-degree photographers, travel enthusiasts, and accessibility advocates, TourCoaster grew from documenting Geneva's lakefront in immersive panoramas to building a global network of guides who share their local knowledge both physically and virtually.

Today, we connect travelers with verified local experts across six continents, offering both in-person guided tours and cutting-edge VR experiences that make the world's most incredible destinations accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

Connect

Bridge the gap between travelers and authentic local experiences through a trusted guide network.

Innovate

Leverage VR and 360-degree technology to create new ways of experiencing destinations.

Include

Make tourism accessible to everyone through inclusive design, VR alternatives, and adaptive experiences.

The Hybrid Approach

Every physical tour has a digital twin.

Our guides don't just lead walking tours — they capture their routes in stunning 360-degree detail, creating VR experiences that serve as both previews for future travelers and complete virtual journeys for those who can't travel in person.

This hybrid model means a hiker in the Swiss Alps and a wheelchair user in Tokyo can share the same guide, the same stories, and the same sense of discovery — just through different mediums.
