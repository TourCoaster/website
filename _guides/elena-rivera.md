Elena grew up exploring Barcelona's winding streets and developed a passion for the city's rich architectural heritage. After studying art history at the University of Barcelona, she became a certified tour guide, specializing in the works of Gaudi and the hidden gems of the Gothic Quarter.

Her tours are known for combining deep historical knowledge with local insider tips — from the best churros con chocolate to secret rooftop viewpoints that most tourists never discover.
