Isabella grew up between the favelas and forests of Rio de Janeiro, developing a unique perspective on the city's extraordinary contrasts. Her tours celebrate both the raw natural beauty of Tijuca — the world's largest urban rainforest — and the pulsing creativity of Rio's street art scene.

Her VR rainforest experiences have been praised for their immersive quality, capturing the sights, sounds, and atmosphere of tropical biodiversity in stunning 360-degree detail.
