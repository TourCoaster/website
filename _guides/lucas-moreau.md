Lucas is a pioneer in combining traditional guided tours with cutting-edge VR technology. His background in photography and passion for Geneva's cultural landscape led him to become one of the first guides to offer fully immersive virtual alternatives to his walking tours.

As a Google Street View contributor, Lucas has documented hundreds of locations across Switzerland, building a vast library of 360-degree content that forms the backbone of TourCoaster's VR experience platform.
