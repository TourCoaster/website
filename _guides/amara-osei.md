Amara combines her background in environmental science with a deep love for the Western Cape's natural beauty. Her hikes are as much about understanding the unique fynbos ecosystem as they are about reaching the summit.

She is passionate about sharing the cultural stories of South Africa's diverse communities, making every trail a journey through both landscape and heritage.
